# Assumptions to validate

The production Command Center remains untouched. Research will reconstruct daily states using only data available as of each historical date.

Initial portfolio policy to test (not yet accepted as final):
- BLUE: highest risk budget; leveraged Nasdaq + V38 stock sleeve
- GREEN: selective risk; smaller leveraged sleeve + larger emphasis on leaders
- YELLOW: no aggressive new risk; reduce leverage, only exceptional stock setups
- RED: defensive; no leveraged Nasdaq and no new individual-stock exposure
- VIX event: observation/arming signal only, never a standalone buy signal
- FTD / regime improvement: staged re-entry trigger

Tax treatment, exact sleeve weights, slippage, contribution timing, loss carryforwards, and stock-selection rules will be parameterized and reported separately.
